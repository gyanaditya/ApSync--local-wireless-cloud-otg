Privacy Policy for ApSync

App Name: ApSync (Package ID: com.gyan.apriync)
Effective Date: November 12, 2026

Dr. Gyanaditya Mourya ("we", "our", or "us") built ApSync as a local-first file synchronization and backup application for Android. This Privacy Policy explains how ApSync handles your information and permissions.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

ApSync operates directly between your Android device and the local servers, external USB OTG drives, or cloud storage accounts you explicitly configure. We do not operate middleman sync servers, and we do not collect, sell, rent, or transmit your personal files, server credentials, Wi-Fi network names, or usage analytics to ourselves or third-party advertisers.

2. Local Storage, Credentials & Biometric Lock

  • Local Configuration: Server host configurations, sync job rules, sync history logs, and credentials (such as SMB, SFTP, FTP, WebDAV passwords, or OAuth tokens) are stored locally on your device using Android's encrypted storage mechanisms. We never send those credentials to our servers.
  • Biometric / Device Lock (Optional): If you enable app lock or credential protection, authentication is processed entirely on-device by the Android operating system (BiometricPrompt). ApSync never accesses, collects, or stores your fingerprint, face, or PIN data.

3. App Permissions & Why They Are Used

  • All Files Access (MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE / Storage): Used solely to read, write, browse, and synchronize files and folders on your device and attached USB OTG storage as configured by you.
  • Location (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION / ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION): Android requires Location permission for apps to read the currently connected Wi-Fi network name (SSID). ApSync uses this strictly on-device to verify your chosen Wi-Fi network condition for Wi-Fi-based sync rules. ApSync never tracks, records, stores, or transmits your physical location.
  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): Used to display foreground sync progress, transfer status, and completion or error alerts.
  • Network, Wi-Fi & Multicast State: Used to connect to your configured LAN/cloud hosts and discover local servers (mDNS/SMB) on your Wi-Fi network.
  • Foreground Service, Wake Lock, Boot Completed & Scheduled Alarms (SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM): Used to keep active transfers running reliably, resume scheduled jobs after device reboot, and trigger user-scheduled sync tasks.

4. Third-Party Services & Cloud Providers

  • Google Play Billing: In-app purchases (ApSync Pro and developer support tips) are processed securely by Google Play. We do not collect or store your payment card details.
  • Cloud Storage Providers (Optional): If you choose to connect third-party cloud providers (such as Google Drive, Dropbox, OneDrive, Box, pCloud, or MEGA), authentication occurs directly between your device and those providers via OAuth2 or their supported protocols, governed by their respective privacy policies.
  • Google API Services User Data Policy: ApSync's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

All app configurations, sync logs, and saved host credentials remain strictly on your device until you remove them. You can delete individual hosts, jobs, or history logs directly inside ApSync, revoke cloud OAuth access from your cloud provider's account settings, or remove all local data at any time by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling ApSync.

6. Children's Privacy

ApSync is not directed to children under the age of 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
