Privacy Policy for Apriync

Effective Date: November 12, 2026

Dr. Gyanaditya Mourya ("we", "our", or "us") built Apriync as a local-first file synchronization and backup application for Android. This Privacy Policy explains how Apriync handles information and permissions when you use the app.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

Apriync operates directly between your Android device and the servers, external USB OTG drives, or cloud storage accounts you explicitly configure. We do not operate middleman sync servers, and we do not collect, sell, rent, or transmit your personal files, server credentials, Wi-Fi SSIDs, or usage analytics to ourselves or third-party advertisers.

2. Local Storage & Credentials

Server host configurations, sync job rules, and credentials (such as SMB, SFTP, FTP, WebDAV passwords, or OAuth tokens) are stored locally on your device using Android's encrypted storage mechanisms. Optional biometric/device PIN authentication is processed entirely on-device by the Android operating system.

3. App Permissions & Why They Are Used

  • All Files Access (MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE / Storage): Used solely to read, write, browse, and synchronize files and folders on your device and attached USB OTG storage as configured by you.
  • Location (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION / ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION): Android requires Location permission for apps to read the currently connected Wi-Fi network name (SSID). Apriync uses this strictly on-device to verify your chosen Wi-Fi network condition before running scheduled or automatic sync jobs. We never track, record, or transmit your physical location.
  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): Used to display foreground sync progress, transfer status, and completion or error alerts.
  • Network, Wi-Fi & Multicast State: Used to connect to your configured LAN/cloud hosts and discover local servers (mDNS/SMB) on your Wi-Fi network.
  • Foreground Service, Wake Lock, Boot Completed & Exact Alarms: Used to keep active transfers running reliably, resume scheduled jobs after device reboot, and trigger user-scheduled syncs.

4. Third-Party Services

  • Google Play Billing: In-app purchases (Apriync Pro and developer support tips) are processed securely by Google Play. We do not collect or store your payment card details.
  • Cloud Storage Providers (Optional): If you choose to connect third-party cloud providers (such as Google Drive, Dropbox, OneDrive, Box, pCloud, or MEGA), authentication occurs directly with those providers via OAuth2 or their official protocols, governed by their respective privacy policies.

5. Children's Privacy

Apriync is not directed to children under the age of 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
