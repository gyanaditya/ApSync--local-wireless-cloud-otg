Privacy Policy for ApSync Effective Date: November 12, 2026 Dr. Gyanaditya Mourya (“we”, “our”, or “us”) built ApSync as a local-first file synchronization and backup application for Android. This Privacy Policy explains how ApSync handles your data and the privacy protections built into the app. Information We Do Not Collect ApSync operates directly between your Android device and the servers, external USB OTG drives, or cloud storage accounts you explicitly configure. We do not operate a middleman service between your device and your storage destinations, and we do not collect your files, credentials, or metadata for central storage or analytics. Local Storage & Credentials Server host configurations, sync job rules, and credentials (such as SMB, SFTP, FTP, WebDAV passwords, or OAuth tokens) are stored locally on your device using Android’s encrypted storage mechanisms. We do not send those credentials to our servers. App Permissions & Why They Are Used All Files Access (MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE / Storage): Used solely to read, write, browse, and synchronize files and folders on your device and attached USB OTG storage as configured by you. Location (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION / ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION): Android requires Location permission for apps to read the currently connected Wi‑Fi network name (SSID). ApSync uses this only to identify the local network for Wi‑Fi-based features. Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): Used to display foreground sync progress, transfer status, and completion or error alerts. Network, Wi‑Fi & Multicast State: Used to connect to your configured LAN/cloud hosts and discover local servers (mDNS/SMB) on your Wi‑Fi network. Foreground Service, Wake Lock, Boot Completed & Exact Alarms: Used to keep active transfers running reliably, resume scheduled jobs after device reboot, and trigger user-scheduled sync tasks. Third-Party Services Google Play Billing: In-app purchases (ApSync Pro and developer support tips) are processed securely by Google Play. We do not collect or store your payment card details. Cloud Storage Providers (Optional): If you choose to connect third-party cloud providers (such as Google Drive, Dropbox, OneDrive, Box, pCloud, or MEGA), authentication occurs directly with those providers. ApSync receives only the minimal access required for the sync operation you configure. Children’s Privacy ApSync is not directed to children under the age of 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Changes to This Privacy Policy We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
